Why Publish is disabled for a half-typed “Publish at”

With scheduling: true, the Publish sheet has a “Publish at” input. A half-typed date and time (validity.badInput) disables Publish, but nothing says why: no line, no aria-invalid, no announcement (#26, WCAG 3.3.1). A time in the past already gets a red line under the input. The proposal shows a reason in that same place, with one new string, scheduleIncomplete. It adds no CSS. Inputs are drawn as text so the half-typed value stays visible on this page.

1. The message

Same node and style as the past-time line. The input gets aria-invalid="true" and already points at the line with aria-describedby. The line goes away as soon as the value is complete or empty, and Publish comes back on. The text is announced once when it appears, like the past-time line.

2. When it appears

Recommended: A. While someone is typing, every date is half-typed for a moment, so a line that appears on the first keystroke (B) would flash red at everyone who fills the field normally. With A the line appears when focus leaves “Publish at” while it is still half-typed (Tab to Publish, or a click elsewhere), and it clears live, on the keystroke that completes or empties the value.
