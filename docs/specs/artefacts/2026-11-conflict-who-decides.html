The diff is now about 2,000 lines. Every round finds a new case of the same thing: the browser decides whether Ben changed the field. To do that it must keep a copy of the document at the right version, and it has to get that right through fetches, refreshes, retries, bulk publish and documents that are deleted and recreated. Round 6 found, among others, that a Keep mine that fails on the network can still overwrite. It also found that a document recreated at version 1 leaves the editor stuck: last round's fix made the browser ignore versions that go down.

Options

A · Ship the narrow fix now; let the server decide later recommended

Back PR #50 down to the original bug. After a conflict catch-up, what a field left open commits is held as a conflict (Keep mine / Take theirs), and a held field never sends except through Keep mine. No copy kept in the browser, no background fetch, and no comparison by field. The publish path and retried saves stay as they are on main today.

New ticket, in the store and contract suite: a field write sent on an old version is accepted when that field didn't change since that version, and refused otherwise. The server has the revisions, so it can tell exactly. Your ruling 1A (only a real change to this field raises a conflict) then holds everywhere, and holds for every save, not only this rare case.

Editor, until the new ticket lands: in the rare case that a field is open while Discard, Restore or Unpublish meets another editor's change, the toast can ask about a field Ben didn't touch. Overwriting unseen is never possible. Site developer: nothing now; later, custom stores must pass the new contract tests.
